# Resonance AI Features Guide

## 🎵 AI Overlay Features (Now Live!)

Your Resonance app now has AI-powered features overlaid on top of YouTube Music!

### How to Access AI Features

#### 1. **Floating AI Button** (Bottom Right)
- Look for the glowing cyan button in the bottom-right corner
- Click it to open the AI Assistant panel
- Hover for tooltip

#### 2. **Keyboard Shortcuts**
- `Cmd/Ctrl + K` - Open AI Search
- `Cmd/Ctrl + P` - Open AI Playlist Generator
- `Escape` - Close any modal

### Available AI Features

#### ⭐ Natural Language Search
**What it does:** Search for music using natural language instead of exact song names

**How to use:**
1. Press `Cmd/Ctrl + K` OR click AI button → "Natural Language Search"
2. Type phrases like:
   - "upbeat songs for Friday afternoon"
   - "chill lo-fi for studying"
   - "90s hip hop workout music"
   - "relaxing jazz for dinner party"
3. Press Enter or click a suggestion

#### ⭐ AI Playlist Generator
**What it does:** Generate custom playlists by describing what you want

**How to use:**
1. Press `Cmd/Ctrl + P` OR click AI button → "AI Playlist Generator"
2. Describe your ideal playlist:
   - "Create a 1-hour playlist of upbeat indie rock for a road trip"
   - "Relaxing acoustic music for Sunday morning"
   - "High-energy EDM for my workout"
3. Click "Generate Playlist"
